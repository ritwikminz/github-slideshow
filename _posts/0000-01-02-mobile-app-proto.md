## Login (UID + Password + Biometric)
- **UID & password** authentication with optional **Face ID / Touch ID**.
- **Remember device** toggle and **Forgot password** flow.
- **Offline-ready** sign-in banner with cached credentials.

## Core Facilities
- **Track FSU milestones** for AWBs.
- **Update AWB data** at terminal doc gate:
  - Gross weight
  - Chargeable weight
  - Dimensions
- **Resend e-AWB** directly from the app.

## Operations Dashboard
- **Success / failure counters** with daily totals.
- **Recent activity** list with status chips.
- **Alerts** for pending or failed transmissions.

## Reliability & UX
- **Offline mode** with queued updates and sync status.
- **Push notifications** for milestone updates and resend results.
- **Clean, modern UI** with large action buttons and quick filters.
